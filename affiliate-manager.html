🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Function: Affiliate sits under Hugo, our Head of Affiliate/Partnerships, driving growth through third-party traffic — affiliates, media buyers, site owners, socials accounts, etc. — rather than owned channels.
  • The Pace: Fast-moving, performance-driven environment. Landscape shifts overnight (traffic sources, compliance, fraud patterns); the team needs to reprioritize fast without losing execution quality.
  • The Bar: Full-funnel ownership — from recruiting and onboarding affiliates through performance analysis, optimization, and troubleshooting. Not a purely relationship or purely analytical role; both are required daily.

⛔️ Non-Negotiables

  1. NSFW comfort — 100% dealbreaker if absent (must be fully comfortable advertising uncensored/adult content)
  2. 5+ years managing affiliate programs, ideally in a fast-scaling or subscription-based business
  3. Hands-on experience with affiliate tracking/network platforms (Everflow or equivalent), plus reporting stacks like Looker Studio, SEMrush
  4. Proven ability to read performance data (traffic, conversions, ROI) and turn it into concrete optimization actions — not just reporting
  5. Has actively recruited and onboarded new affiliates/partners, not only managed an existing book
  6. Experienced with diverse acquisition channels (SEO, media buying, paid social) even if not hands-on executing all of them
  7. Fluent English communicator
  8. Fully committed — no concurrent jobs/projects

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Purpose: use the first interview to determine whether the candidate has meaningful affiliate management experience, measurable commercial results, motivation, and communication skills required to progress. Include a summary of answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

❓ Interview Questions

  1. What is your current situation, and why are you considering a move right now?
  2. What specifically attracted you to this opportunity with EverAI?
  3. Did you review Candy.ai and the Handbook?
  4. Did you test the product? How far did you go — did you create a character, chat, try different features, or mainly browse the website?
  5. Have you worked with NSFW or adult content in a professional context before?
  6. Are you fully comfortable recruiting and managing affiliates who promote explicit or adult content?
  7. In your last role, what were your actual goals, and what was the primary metric used to determine whether you were performing well?
  8. What is your biggest achievement from the past 6–12 months?
  9. How many active affiliates did you personally manage, and what results did your portfolio generate?
  10. How many affiliates have you personally recruited vs. inherited or simply managed? What share of your portfolio did you personally source, and how?
  11. How did you activate new affiliates, and what did you do to retain/fidelize your top performers?
  12. Walk me through a deal you negotiated — CPA, RevShare, hybrid, or flat. How did you decide the structure?
  13. How do you manage portfolio economics, traffic/source quality, and fraud?
  14. What were your growth targets, did you hit them, and what was your typical month-over-month growth?
  15. What's been your biggest growth bottleneck, and how did you work around it?
  16. Which affiliate/traffic-source types have performed best for you (e.g. SEO, media buyers, native, paid social, webmasters, comparison sites, PPC, mailing)?
  17. If you were acquiring users for Candy.ai specifically, where would you start?
  18. Which affiliate/network relationships do you currently have active?
  19. Which metrics did you monitor regularly, and what decisions did they help you make?
  20. What are your future career goals?
  21. Why did you leave each of your last two or three roles?
  22. What questions do you have for me?
