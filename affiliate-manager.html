🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Product: Candy.ai / EverAI — candidates must be 100% comfortable promoting products built on uncensored, adult-oriented AI models and content.
  • The Function: Affiliate sits under Hugo, our Head of Affiliate/Partnerships, driving growth through third-party traffic — affiliates, media buyers, site owners, socials accounts, etc. — rather than owned channels.
  • The Pace: Fast-moving, performance-driven environment. Landscape shifts overnight (traffic sources, compliance, fraud patterns); the team needs to reprioritize fast without losing execution quality.
  • The Bar: Full-funnel ownership — from recruiting and onboarding affiliates through performance analysis, optimization, and troubleshooting. Not a purely relationship or purely analytical role; both are required daily.

🎯 Calibration Anchors & Target Profiles

Ideal background:

  • 5+ years managing affiliate programs, ideally in a fast-scaling or subscription-based business.
  • Hands-on experience with affiliate tracking/network platforms (Everflow or equivalent), plus reporting stacks like Looker Studio, SEMrush.
  • Proven ability to read performance data (traffic, conversions, ROI) and turn it into concrete optimization actions — not just reporting.
  • Has actively recruited and onboarded new affiliates/partners, not only managed an existing book.
  • Experienced with diverse acquisition channels (SEO, media buying, paid social) even if not hands-on executing all of them.
  • Strong Excel/Sheets fluency for independent data analysis.
  • Independent decision-maker — confident, sharp, has "been through things" (real challenges/scars, not a clean-path career).

📁 Ideal source industries

Adult and iGaming/Casino — closest match on affiliate model, NSFW/high-scrutiny content comfort, aggressive performance culture, and exposure to fraud/compliance issues.

Also strong fits, sharing similar DNA (performance-driven, compliance/fraud exposure, affiliate-network growth):

  • Dating apps / online dating — closest analog to companionship products; similar funnels and delicate-content handling
  • Sports betting — same aggressive CPA/CPL models, same fraud/compliance exposure
  • Nutra / health & wellness (info products, supplements) — heavily affiliate-driven, high-scrutiny ad content, experience with platform bans/appeals
  • Forex / CFDs / crypto trading platforms — high-CPA affiliate models, heavy regulatory scrutiny
  • VPN / subscription SaaS with aggressive affiliate programs — subscription-model fluency (recurring revenue, churn) plus affiliate network experience
  • Mobile gaming (F2P) UA/affiliate roles — strong overlap on scaling affiliate/network programs and data-driven optimization
  • CBD / vape / other "restricted ad category" products — used to working around platform ad restrictions and alternative traffic sourcing

⛔️ Non-Negotiables

  1. NSFW comfort — 100% dealbreaker if absent (must be fully comfortable advertising uncensored/adult content)
  2. 5+ years affiliate marketing/management experience
  3. Hands-on experience with affiliate platforms/networks (Everflow-type tools) and reporting tools (Looker Studio, SEMrush or equivalent)
  4. Fluent English communicator
  5. Fully committed — no concurrent jobs/projects
  6. Independent and confident — able to make own calls without heavy oversight

📋 Evaluation Framework

1. Technical & Analytical Depth — 35%

Can speak concretely to affiliate platform setup, tracking, and reporting. Uses data to drive decisions, not just describe results. Comfortable with Excel/Sheets-level analysis independently.

Hard Filters: Can't explain specific tools/platforms used; relationship-only background with no data ownership; no concrete optimization examples.

2. Ownership, Recruiting & Growth — 25%

Has actively sourced and recruited new affiliates/partners (not just managed inbound). Can describe scaling a program, not only maintaining one. Troubleshoots issues independently, makes calls without waiting for approval.

Hard Filters: Purely account-management background with no recruitment/growth activity; no examples of scaling a network; needs constant direction/approval.

3. Mindset & Culture Fit — 20%

Doer mindset — balances execution, planning, and strategy. Adaptable to overnight shifts in priorities. Confident, sharp, humble, coachable, direct communicator. Has real war stories — disputes, fraud, volume crashes — and owned the resolution.

Hard Filters: Rigid/process-only mindset; poor English; low energy; resistant to feedback; no evidence of having navigated real adversity.

4. Bonus / Growth Signals — 20%

Experience with S2S postback tracking, ad servers, fraud prevention, A/B testing/funnel optimization, and subscription-based business models.

💬 Recruiter Screening Brief

ℹ️

Purpose: use the first interview to determine whether the candidate has meaningful affiliate management experience, measurable commercial results, motivation, and communication skills required to progress. Include a summary of answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

📋 The Interview Should Assess

  • Recent achievements and measurable results
  • Personal ownership of affiliate relationships and portfolio performance
  • Ability to recruit, activate, and grow affiliates
  • Experience negotiating commercial models and terms
  • Understanding of relevant metrics and traffic quality
  • Motivation for joining EverAI
  • Career goals and future direction
  • Communication quality
  • Reasons for leaving previous companies
  • Risks, inconsistencies, and red flags
  • Quality and relevance of the candidate's questions

❓ Interview Questions

  1. What is your current situation, and why are you considering a move right now?
  2. What specifically attracted you to this opportunity with EverAI?
  3. Did you review Candy.ai and the Handbook?
  4. Did you test the product? How far did you go — did you create a character, chat, try different features, or mainly browse the website?
  5. Have you worked with NSFW or adult content in a professional context before?
  6. Are you fully comfortable recruiting and managing affiliates who promote explicit or adult content?
  7. In your last role, what were your actual goals, and what was the primary metric used to determine whether you were performing well?
  8. What is your biggest achievement from the past 6–12 months?
  9. How many active affiliates did you personally manage, and what results did your portfolio generate?
  10. Which metrics did you monitor regularly, and what decisions did they help you make?
  11. What are your future career goals?
  12. Why did you leave each of your last two or three roles?
  13. What questions do you have for me?
🔎

Interview Recommendations

  • Dig deeper into vague or generic answers
  • Do not feed candidates the expected answers
  • Require specific goals, actions, metrics, and measurable outcomes
  • Focus particularly on achievements from the past 6–12 months
  • Ask candidates to summarize or use shorter sentences when their answers become long or unclear
  • Investigate their reasons for leaving previous companies and any inconsistencies in their career history
  • Assess whether their questions are relevant and demonstrate genuine interest
  • Confirm whether affiliates were personally sourced and managed or inherited
  • Focus on active affiliates rather than total registered affiliates
  • Distinguish individual portfolio results from company-wide revenue
  • Request specific examples of commercial terms they personally negotiated
  • Determine how affiliates were activated after signing
  • Look for evidence of growing existing partners, not only recruiting them
  • Ask specifically which tools they use daily for tracking and reporting (e.g. Everflow, Looker Studio, SEMrush) and have them walk through one — don't accept a generic "I know tracking" answer
  • Determine whether they maintain relevant industry relationships
  • Confirm that their experience is genuine affiliate management rather than only paid media, sales, account management, or influencer marketing
