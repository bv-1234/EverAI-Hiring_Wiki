🎯 Role Mission

Own and scale adult acquisition at significant spend while protecting profitability, subscription quality and long-term customer value.

📋 Responsibilities

  • Own adult acquisition campaigns end to end.
  • Control and reallocate substantial campaign budgets.
  • Scale profitable traffic sources, placements and creatives.
  • Evaluate performance through CPA, rebill, LTV and payback.
  • Diagnose tracking, fraud, placement and funnel problems.
  • Build structured creative-testing systems.
  • Define testing budgets, winner rules and stopping rules.
  • Guide creative production using performance evidence.
  • Identify new traffic sources and expansion opportunities.
  • Communicate strategy, decisions, results and risks clearly.

⛔️ Screening Criteria

  • Is personally running and optimizing campaigns now.
  • Personally controls or drives meaningful acquisition commercially — via platform spend, flat/direct deals, publisher/network relationships, or partnership-driven traffic.
  • Can show recent measurable commercial impact.
  • Personally participates in creative testing and iteration.
💡

$300K+/month personally controlled spend is NOT a hard gate for this track. The Adult team works substantially through flat deals, direct deals, traffic-source relationships, publisher/network relationships, and partnership/business development — not only standard paid-media buying. Do not reject an excellent Adult candidate simply because they don't fit a standard monthly-spend model.

Direct adult/RTB experience is strongly preferred but not mandatory. Candidates may also qualify from other sensitive/restricted/grey verticals — iGaming, casino, betting, dating, crypto, sweepstakes, nutra, or similar restricted-acquisition environments — where they show they understand harder acquisition environments and can operate commercially within them. Mainstream-only performance-marketing experience remains a weaker fit for this track.

Also confirms current operating experience (still close to execution — strategy/advisory/account-management-only is not suitable), proven company experience at meaningful scale, personal campaign ownership, and product-category alignment (fully comfortable with uncensored products and explicit advertising creatives).

🚩 Red Flags

🚩
  • Team/company spend presented as personal spend.
  • Vague "performance improved" with no numbers.
  • No current hands-on execution — direct media buying is no longer recent.
  • Creative entirely owned by another team, or decisions depend entirely on account-manager recommendations.
  • Profile is supply-side/publisher-side, or affiliate/account management without direct media buying.
  • Profile is mainstream-only (Meta/Google/TikTok) with no meaningful RTB/native experience.
  • Commercial reasoning stops at clicks or CTR; results cannot be personally attributed.
  • Seniority masks distance from campaign execution.

💬 Recruiter Screening Questions

  1. What is your current situation, and why are you considering a move right now?
  2. What specifically attracted you to this opportunity with EverAI?
  3. Did you review Candy.ai and the Handbook?
    • What did you notice, and how far did you test the product?
  4. Are you fully comfortable working with explicit content as part of your day-to-day role?
    • Have you worked with NSFW or adult content in a professional context before?
  5. In your last role at [company], what were your actual goals, and what was the primary metric used to determine whether you were performing well?
  6. What is your biggest growth / technical achievement from the past 6–12 months?
    • What measurable business impact did this achievement have?
    • What was the actual performance metric, and was it a company goal?
  7. Which platforms do you personally manage today, and what monthly budget or deal volume do you directly control?
    • If unclear: How much of that is personally yours vs. team / portfolio / company spend?
  8. Walk me through your creative-testing process.
    • What decisions do you personally make?
    • How do you decide what to scale or kill?
  9. Give me one recent example where you changed spend or campaign strategy based on CAC / CPA / ROAS / LTV / payback.
    • What did you change?
    • What happened?
  10. What are your future career goals?
  11. Why did you leave each of your last two or three roles?
  12. Location / compensation expectations / notice period / B2B?
  13. What questions do you have for me?

🎯 Track-Specific Guidance

Priority profiles

  • Adult / RTB / native media buyers
  • iGaming / casino / betting performance teams
  • Dating, crypto, sweepstakes, nutra, or similar restricted-acquisition profiles
  • Mobile gaming candidates with genuine RTB/native/programmatic buying
  • Performance agencies where the candidate personally buys media

Strong signals (beyond spend): direct publisher/network relationships, negotiating flat or direct deals, partnership development, source-quality evaluation, commercial/funnel optimization, managing harder or restricted traffic. Advertiser-side buying across adult networks; native, push, display or popunder acquisition; placement-level optimization; traffic-quality and fraud analysis; rebill and subscription-economics experience; cohort analysis; flat-deal negotiation with publishers; international acquisition.

Adult-industry or adtech experience without current advertiser-side execution is insufficient. Example of ideal/good candidate for calibration: this LinkedIn profile.

📁 Sourcing Guidance

Relevant vocabulary (not a hard requirement — useful for recognizing genuine experience): TrafficJunky, TrafficStars, ExoClick, Clickadu, PropellerAds, Adsterra, EroAdvertising, TrafficFactory, Adnium, Voluum, Binom, RedTrack, S2S postbacks, sub-IDs, click IDs, CPA, FTD, cohort payback, LTV/CAC.
