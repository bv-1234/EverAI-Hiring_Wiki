🎯 Role Mission

Own and scale adult acquisition at significant spend while protecting profitability, subscription quality and long-term customer value.

📋 Responsibilities

  • Own adult acquisition campaigns end to end.
  • Control and reallocate substantial campaign budgets.
  • Scale profitable traffic sources, placements and creatives.
  • Evaluate performance through CPA, rebill, LTV and payback.
  • Diagnose tracking, fraud, placement and funnel problems.
  • Build structured creative-testing systems.
  • Define testing budgets, winner rules and stopping rules.
  • Guide creative production using performance evidence.
  • Identify new traffic sources and expansion opportunities.
  • Communicate strategy, decisions, results and risks clearly.

⛔️ Required Profile

  1. 4+ years in paid acquisition — relevant experience must come from hands-on performance marketing, media buying or user acquisition, not marketing experience generally.
  2. Current operating experience — still close to campaign execution. Strategy-only, advisory-only and account-management-only profiles are not suitable.
  3. Proven company experience — must have operated within a company or directly managed campaigns for clients at meaningful scale. Freelance-only experience without clear scaled execution is insufficient.
  4. Personal campaign ownership — able to explain which platforms, campaigns and optimization decisions they personally own.
  5. Personal commercial control — currently or recently controlled meaningful acquisition spend or deal volume, whether through platform buying, flat deals, direct publisher/network deals, or partnership-driven traffic. Team budget, client portfolio value and company revenue do not count unless the candidate personally controlled allocation and optimization. A fixed monthly-spend number is not a hard gate for this track — see the note below.
  6. Measurable commercial impact — connects their actions to acquisition outcomes such as CAC, ROAS, LTV, payback, retention, revenue or contribution margin.
  7. Creative-testing ownership — personally developed hypotheses, launched creative tests, interpreted results and determined the next action.
  8. Product-category alignment — fully comfortable working daily with uncensored products and explicit advertising creatives.
  9. Fluent English — communicates clearly and concisely in an international remote environment.

✨ Strong Profile Signals

These signals increase sourcing priority but are not independent proof of qualification:

  • Named experience with Meta, TikTok, Google Ads/UAC, Snapchat, native advertising or other paid-acquisition channels.
  • Experience across several paid channels rather than one narrow platform.
  • Landing-page, funnel or conversion-rate optimization.
  • Tracking and attribution experience using tools such as GA4, AppsFlyer, Adjust or Voluum.
  • Explicit budget, acquisition or commercial results on the profile.
  • Experience handling advertising restrictions, compliance issues or account bans.
  • Two or more years at one company with increasing responsibility.
  • Progression from individual contributor into lead or management while remaining hands-on.
  • Experience at a fast-growing, high-volume or performance-led company.
  • Experience in one of the priority industries.
💡

$300K+/month personally controlled spend is NOT a hard gate for this track. The Adult team works substantially through flat deals, direct deals, traffic-source relationships, publisher/network relationships, and partnership/business development — not only standard paid-media buying. Do not reject an excellent Adult candidate simply because they don't fit a standard monthly-spend model.

Direct adult/RTB experience is strongly preferred but not mandatory. Candidates may also qualify from other sensitive/restricted/grey verticals — iGaming, casino, betting, dating, crypto, sweepstakes, nutra, or similar restricted-acquisition environments — where they show they understand harder acquisition environments and can operate commercially within them. Mainstream-only performance-marketing experience remains a weaker fit for this track.

🎯 Track-Specific Sourcing Guidance

Priority profiles

  • Adult / RTB / native media buyers
  • iGaming / casino / betting performance teams
  • Dating, crypto, sweepstakes, nutra, or similar restricted-acquisition profiles
  • Mobile gaming candidates with genuine RTB/native/programmatic buying
  • Performance agencies where the candidate personally buys media

Strong signals (beyond spend): direct publisher/network relationships, negotiating flat or direct deals, partnership development, source-quality evaluation, commercial/funnel optimization, managing harder or restricted traffic.

Relevant vocabulary (not a hard requirement — useful for recognizing genuine experience): TrafficJunky, TrafficStars, ExoClick, Clickadu, PropellerAds, Adsterra, EroAdvertising, TrafficFactory, Adnium, Voluum, Binom, RedTrack, S2S postbacks, sub-IDs, click IDs, CPA, FTD, cohort payback, LTV/CAC.

🚩

Do not submit for Adult RTB if

  • Profile is supply-side / publisher-side.
  • Profile is affiliate/account management without direct media buying.
  • Profile is mainstream-only Meta/Google/TikTok with no meaningful RTB/native experience.
  • RTB buying is stale.
  • Quoted numbers are team/client portfolio values rather than personally controlled spend.

⛔️ Senior Performance Marketer — Do Not Submit Unless All Are True

  1. Currently personally running and optimizing paid-acquisition campaigns.
  2. Approximately 4+ years of meaningful paid acquisition / performance marketing experience — or equivalent commercial depth in adult/RTB, iGaming, betting, dating, crypto, sweepstakes, or nutra.
  3. Personally controls or drives meaningful acquisition commercially — via platform spend, flat/direct deals, or partnership-driven traffic. No fixed monthly-spend number is required for this track.
  4. Can show a recent, quantified commercial result.
  5. Personally owns campaign decisions.
  6. Personally participates in creative testing and iteration.
  7. Has relevant B2C / DTC / consumer acquisition experience.
  8. Fully comfortable working with explicit / NSFW content.
  9. Accepts the B2B / contractor setup.

What does NOT count as personally managed spend

  • Total company spend
  • Total team spend
  • Agency/client portfolio spend
  • Publisher revenue
  • Affiliate revenue
  • Spend executed by junior media buyers or another team
⚠️

Years in marketing ≠ years in paid acquisition. General marketing, product marketing, partnerships, social media, account management, affiliate management, or demand generation do not count toward the Senior paid-acquisition bar unless the candidate personally operated acquisition campaigns.

Reject when

  • Direct media buying is no longer recent.
  • Spend represents revenue or portfolio value.
  • Campaign execution is delegated.
  • Results cannot be personally attributed.
  • Creative decisions depend entirely on account-manager recommendations.
  • Commercial reasoning stops at clicks or CTR.
  • Seniority masks distance from campaign execution.
  • They are not comfortable with explicit creatives.
  • They do not accept the contract arrangement.
