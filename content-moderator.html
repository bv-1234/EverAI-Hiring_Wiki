🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Team: Sits on the Compliance Team. Day-to-day, works closely with the Senior Content Moderator; the function overall reports to Maxwelle Sokol (Legal & Safety Lead).
  • The Role: Reviews and approves/rejects user-generated content (video, image, text) against platform standards, documenting the basis for each decision. Runs safety/compliance reviews on in-house content and workflows (e.g. datasets, AI characters). Acts on content flagged by internal detection systems, and tags/categorizes content for correct visibility across the platform.

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

  1. Our platform is an adult-content AI companionship product — you'd be reviewing and discussing sexually explicit material and adult themes daily, as the core of the job, not incidentally.
    • Are you fully comfortable with that?
    • Have you worked with sensitive content before — NSFW, or CSAM-adjacent detection/removal/DMCA work? (Not required — a bonus if so.)
  2. This work can be psychologically demanding over time. How do you personally manage exposure to disturbing content?
  3. Walk me through your content moderation experience — what platform, what type of content, what volume, and what tools did you use?
  4. What was your typical throughput and accuracy on that queue — roughly how many items did you review per hour or per shift, and what was your error rate, if it was tracked?
  5. Tell me about a specific piece of borderline or ambiguous content you had to make a call on. How did you interpret the guideline, what did you decide, and how did you document it?
  6. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a guideline or a manager's call on a piece of content — what did you do?
  7. Why are you looking to leave your current role?
  8. What questions do you have for me?
