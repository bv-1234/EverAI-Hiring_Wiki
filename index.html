        🏢 About EverAI
        

        “I've never heard of EverAI.”
        EverAI is the company behind Candy.ai, one of the top AI web products worldwide.
        “Is the company stable?”
        Profitable, with 50M+ users. Founded in 2023 and growing fast.
        “I'm not sure about adult content.”
        Safety is built in: EverGuard, our own AI moderation system, plus independent external audits, board oversight, and regular partner and payment-provider audits. Investor due diligence is complete. If they're still unsure, don't push.
        “What do I put on LinkedIn?”
        List EverAI as the employer. In anything public (LinkedIn profile, posts, job ads) we only say EverAI, never Candy.ai. In private messages and calls, mentioning Candy.ai is fine.
        
    
        
    
        💶 Pay and contract
        

        “Is the salary gross? Does it include a bonus?”
        Every range in this wiki is the total package: gross per year, including any variable. Marketing roles are fixed + 20–30% variable, paid quarterly.
        “Is there equity, or a bonus on top?”
        Not part of the standard package. Ask your TA contact.
        “What contract would I get?”
        B2B is preferred: they invoice us as a freelancer or through their own company. When B2B isn't possible, EOR through Deel (not for Marketing roles right now).
        “Which currency am I paid in?”
        Local currency, USD or EUR.
        “Do you sponsor visas?”
        No. They need the right to work where they live, outside the blocked countries.

        “How does fully remote work there?”
        About 100 people across the world, a co-working budget, and team gatherings every year, including CandyCon.
        “What are the benefits?”
        4 weeks paid time off, $100/month towards health insurance, unlimited sessions with psychologists and coaches (OpenUp), a learning budget, co-working visits, equipment budget and AI tools. Full list in the Guide.
        
    
        
    
        🗓️ Interviews and start
        

        “What's the interview process?”
        Usually 3–4 weeks: recruiter call → hiring manager → case study → case study interview → culture fit → references → offer. Each brief shows its exact steps.
        “Is the case study paid? How long does it take?”
        It depends on the role. Your TA contact shares the details when the candidate reaches that stage.

        Ideal notice is up to 1 month. Over 2 months: flag it to your TA contact before submitting. Contract onboarding through Deel takes 3–5 business days.

        Send them the Candidate Handbook ↗, and see Candidate prep.

        Candidate prep
        

        Candidate prep
        Send every candidate the Candidate Handbook: it covers our interview stages and how to prepare. Before they interview, make sure they:
        

        • ✓Quantify their results: “conversion from 1.2% to 2.8% in 3 months by testing X”, not “I improved conversion”
        • ✓Can say what they did personally, not the team, and what changed because of them
        • ✓Have 2–3 success stories and one failure ready: situation, action, result, and what they learned
        • ✓Have tried Candy.ai and can explain why this space interests them
        

        Interview process (usually 3–4 weeks): recruiter call → hiring manager → case study → case study interview → culture fit → references → offer. Some roles differ; each brief shows its exact steps. After you submit, TA runs the process and all candidate communication.
