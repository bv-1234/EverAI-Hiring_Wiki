🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Scale: High-traffic, production-grade systems serving millions of users. This is not a side project or internal tool — engineering decisions have immediate product consequences.
  • The Stack: Primary backend stack is Ruby on Rails.
  • The Team: Fully remote, cross-functional engineering team (4 squads). Engineers work closely with PMs, Designers, QA, and AI/DevOps — no silos, no narrow lanes.
  • The Bar: Everyone is hands-on. Ownership is expected from day one. Engineers take initiative, make decisions, and solve hard problems without waiting for permission.

🎯 Calibration Anchors & Target Profiles

What matters is what the candidate actually built and owned in production Ruby on Rails — not the size or fame of the company they built it at. Agency, consulting, freelance, startup, and smaller product-company backgrounds are all valid; none of them are weaker by default than a big-name employer.

  • Recent, hands-on production Ruby on Rails work — their daily reality, not a side project or occasional task required
  • Experience with monolithic architecture and/or scaling challenges strong positive signal
  • Comfortable in a startup environment: ambiguity, fast iteration, direct communication strong positive signal
  • High-traffic B2C/SaaS exposure, broad product responsibility, scale-up experience, or strong career progression positive signal, not required
🆕

Industries that don't transfer well
Healthcare, government, and similarly regulated or slow-moving environments. Candidates from these backgrounds rarely adapt to our pace and culture.

⛔️ Non-Negotiables

  1. NSFW comfort — 100% dealbreaker if absent
  2. Recent, hands-on production experience with Ruby on Rails (5+ years backend engineering overall; other modern stacks considered only with strong, equivalent recent depth)
  3. Production experience with high-traffic, scalable systems — not just CRUD apps or small internal tools
  4. Fully committed — no multiple concurrent jobs or projects
  5. Based in Europe, with CET ±3 working-hours overlap
  6. Strong English, spoken and written
  7. AI tool usage — mandatory, hard requirement, not a nice-to-have. This is one of only 4 roles company-wide (with Product Designer, Senior Product Manager, and AI Cinematic Video Editor/Creator) where active AI usage in the day-to-day workflow is a genuine hard gate rather than a positive signal. Candidates must currently use AI development tools (e.g. Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, ChatGPT) and must be able to inspect, correct, and explain AI-generated code — not just accept it. No AI usage at all is an immediate no.

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

  1. Can you briefly walk me through your current role and main responsibilities?
  2. What is the biggest technical challenge you faced in the last 6 months? What options did you weigh, and why did you choose the approach you did?
  3. Why are you considering a move now? (If applicable) You've had some leadership exposure — why are you interested in a Senior IC position rather than continuing in management?
  4. Tell me about a time you had to debug or verify something quickly, working independently.
  5. Have you tried Candy.ai? What do you think about it?
  6. Our flagship product involves AI companionship and adult-oriented content. Are you fully comfortable working in an environment that can include NSFW or adult themes, handled respectfully and professionally?
  7. What are the top 2–3 things you're looking for in your next opportunity?
  8. Do you use AI tools in your development workflow? Which ones, and how do you verify or correct what they produce before it ships?
  9. How do you feel about working in a fast-moving startup that is scaling quickly?
  10. What are your compensation expectations for this role?
