# Internal TA Archive

Preserved internal-only, historical, and operational context removed from the shared EverAI Partner Hiring Wiki so the live wiki stays focused on current, partner-safe recruiting guidance.

**How to use this file:** nothing here is meant for agencies/partners. Each entry records the source page, exactly what was removed, and why. If you need historical context, HM calibration history, or past diagnostic reasoning, it lives here rather than being deleted outright.

Scope note: this archive covers the operational TA/partner wiki pages only (Home, Open Roles, Ashby, Hiring Process, role briefs, etc.). It does **not** cover the candidate-facing Handbook mirror also hosted in this repo (`handbook.html`, `who-we-are.html`, `what-we-do.html`, `engineering.html`, `everai-way.html`, `get-in-touch.html`, `privacy-notice.html`, `recruitment-process.html`, `safety-and-ethics.html`, `under-the-hood.html`, `why-join-us.html`) — that content is already candidate/partner-safe by nature and was out of scope for this cleanup.
