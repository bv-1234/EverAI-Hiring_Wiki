  • The Team: Embedded in R&D, working on Candy.ai's content pipeline (MPC / Companion Mode). This is a deeply technical creative role — not a standard editing position. Candidate works with high autonomy on execution and technical decisions.

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

  1. Walk me through your current workflow — what does a typical production look like from brief to delivery?
  2. Which AI video generation tools are you actively using right now, and what are you using them for?
  3. Share a piece of AI-generated long-form content you're proud of — what were the technical and creative challenges?
  4. How do you maintain character and scene consistency across a longer AI-generated narrative sequence?
  5. Have you tried Candy.ai? Our product operates in the adult AI companionship space — are you fully comfortable working with explicit NSFW content on a daily basis?
    1. Do you have previous experience working in the adult space?
  6. Do you use ComfyUI? If yes, walk me through a workflow you've built. (Bonus signal — not a filter.)
  7. If you were able to create anything you wanted without budget constraints or rules, but simply for your pleasure, what would it be?
