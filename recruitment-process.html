What You Can Expect

We respect your time and energy. Our recruitment process is designed to be rigorous yet efficient, ensuring we find the best A-players while giving you a transparent look into how we work.

While the specific steps may vary slightly depending on the role, here is the general roadmap of your journey with EverAI:

📄

Step 1: Application Review
After your application, a member of our team will review your application personally. We look for impact, speed, and alignment with our values.

🤝

Step 2: Culture & Discovery Call

Who: A member of our Talent Acquisition team.

Goal: To get to know you! We'll discuss your background, your interest in EverAI, and answer your initial questions about the company. We want to ensure our culture of speed and ownership is a match for what you are looking for.

🧠

Step 3: Hiring Manager(s) Deep Dive

Who: Your potential future manager or a colleague from the team.

Goal: A deeper conversation about your expertise. We'll dig into your past projects, the challenges you've solved, and the "wins" you are most proud of. This is your chance to geek out on your craft.

💻

Step 4: The Challenge / Case Study

What: A practical assessment or take-home task.

Goal: We believe in "Show, Don't Tell." Whether it's a coding challenge, a design task, or a strategic case study, this step allows you to showcase your skills in a real-world context similar to the work you'd do here.

🚀

Step 5: Founder Round / Cultural Fit Interview

Who: Key stakeholders, cross-functional peers, or our Co-Founders.

Goal: The final sanity check. We assess "Culture Add": how you will raise the bar for the team, and alignment on our long-term vision.

🎉

Step 6: Reference Check & Offer
Goal: We speak to your former peers/managers to understand how to set you up for success (don't worry, we will never contact anyone without your permission). Then, if it's a match, we roll out the red carpet with an offer to join the rocket ship!

📹

A Note on AI Interview Recordings
To support a consistent and fair hiring process in our remote-first environment, we use AI note-taking tools during interviews so interviewers can focus on the conversation rather than manual note-taking. Consent is requested where required, and recording is always visible during the interview. Rest assured, these recordings are strictly confidential, used solely for hiring decisions, and are permanently deleted after 4 months, in accordance with our Applicant Privacy Notice.

How to Ace the Interview

Most candidates don't fail interviews because they lack intelligence or relevant experience; they fail because they lack precision. In a high-velocity environment, we don't just value talent; we value reliability.

The candidates who perform best communicate clearly, focus on outcomes, and remove unnecessary detail.

Here is how to stand out in your interview process:

🎯

Prioritize Precision over Explanation

Don't just tell us what you did; tell us why it mattered. When using the STAR Method, keep the "Situation" brief and spend 80% of your time on Actions and Results.

  • Stay Objective: Focus on outcomes, not emotions.
  • Quantify Everything: Did you increase retention by 15%? Did you cut latency by 200ms? Numbers are the language of trust.
⚡

Show Your Velocity
One of our core values is "Aim Bigger, Go Faster." We look for a bias toward action. Share examples of when you chose "done" over "perfect" or iterated on a project in record time. Show us you can handle the speed of a remote, high-growth team.

📢

Be Concise & Direct

In a remote, high-speed environment, clear communication is a superpower.

  • Be Concise: If your answer feels long, it is. Keep it sharp.
  • Kill the Acronyms: Speak plainly. Clarity is more impressive than sounding technical.
  • The "I Don't Know" Rule: If you're stuck, don't ramble. Say: "I don't know the answer to that yet, but here is exactly how I would find out."
🕵️

Ask Genuine & High-Signal Questions
Skip the generic, ChatGPT-generated questions. We want to see real curiosity about Candy.ai. Ask us about our roadmap, our specific technical debt, or the biggest bottleneck we're facing this quarter. Show us you've already started thinking like an employee.

✨

Be Authentic & Grounded
We hire people, not resumes. You don't need to be a "perfect" candidate; you need to be a precise one. Bring your unique perspective, your real challenges, and your honest experiences; failures included.

😮‍💨

Take a Breath, You've Got This!
If you encounter a tricky question, take a moment to gather your thoughts. Thoughtfulness and confidence always leave a good impression.
