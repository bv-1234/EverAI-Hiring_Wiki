🎯 Role Mission

Operate and optimize acquisition campaigns across adult, native, push and RTB traffic sources.

The person will make evidence-based campaign decisions and progressively take ownership of larger budgets.

📋 Responsibilities

  • Launch and optimize adult acquisition campaigns.
  • Monitor performance by traffic source, placement, format, market and creative.
  • Reallocate spend based on CPA, conversion and traffic quality.
  • Identify weak placements, fraud and low-quality traffic.
  • Contribute to creative hypotheses and testing plans.
  • Evaluate performance beyond clicks and registrations.
  • Analyze subscription conversion and customer quality.
  • Maintain accurate campaign tracking and reporting.
  • Communicate results and recommended actions clearly.
  • Develop toward independent ownership of substantial campaign budgets.

⛔️ Required Profile

  1. Recent, direct campaign execution.
  2. Personal responsibility for campaigns or a defined portion of spend.
  3. Evidence of making optimization decisions.
  4. Understanding of CPA, conversion, ROAS and basic LTV.
  5. Meaningful involvement in creative testing.
  6. Strong numerical reasoning.
  7. Clear communication.
  8. Fast learning and high ownership.
  9. B2C or DTC experience.
  10. Full comfort working directly with explicit creatives.
  11. Acceptance of the B2B contractor arrangement.

There is no fixed minimum budget for Junior candidates. The candidate must still prove real execution and decision ownership.

🎯 Track-Specific Sourcing Guidance

Target profiles should show:

  • Direct RTB/native/push buying.
  • Campaign setup and optimization.
  • Tracking/postbacks.
  • Traffic-quality analysis.
  • Creative testing involvement.

⛔️ Junior Performance Marketer — Do Not Submit Unless All Are True

  1. Has direct campaign exposure and works inside the ad platforms.
  2. Can explain what they personally changed or optimized.
  3. Understands core metrics and can connect metrics to actions.
  4. Can separate their own contribution from the wider team.
  5. Shows learning speed and creative/testing curiosity.
  6. Fully comfortable working with explicit / NSFW content.
  7. Accepts the B2B / contractor setup.
⚠️

Do not apply the Senior $300K/month spend bar to Junior candidates. For Junior, the spend question establishes level of ownership and exposure, not a hard minimum.

🏷️ Relevant Adult RTB Experience

Relevant platforms and tools include:

  • TrafficJunky, ExoClick, TrafficStars, Clickadu, PropellerAds, Adsterra, TrafficFactory.
  • Taboola, Outbrain, MGID.
  • Voluum, Binom, RedTrack.
  • Server-to-server postbacks, sub-ID and placement-level tracking.

Direct experience with every platform is not required.

Experience at an adult advertising network, publisher or adtech company is relevant only when the candidate personally bought and optimized traffic from the advertiser side.

⚠️

TrafficJunky and TrafficStars are Off-Limits Companies. This applies to candidates currently employed by those networks — not to candidates who simply bought traffic on their platforms as an advertiser. Do not approach current employees of TrafficJunky or TrafficStars for this or any role; see the Off-Limits Companies page.

🚩

Reject when

  • Experience is limited to reporting or campaign coordination.
  • Another person made and implemented the campaign decisions.
  • Experience is mainly account management, partnerships or publisher monetization.
  • The candidate cannot explain how traffic quality was evaluated.
  • Their reasoning stops at clicks or CTR.
  • They have no meaningful involvement in creative testing.
  • They are not comfortable handling explicit creatives.
  • They do not accept the contract arrangement.
