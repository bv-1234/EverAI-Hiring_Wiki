We are looking for a hands-on Security Engineer to own application security, GRC, and security awareness. This isn't a compliance-only seat — you'll code-review, drive vulnerability remediation, build out the risk register, coordinate external audits/pentests, and readiness to carry weekend monitoring coverage when needed, with real ownership from day one.

🔞

Daily work involves NSFW exposure on Candy.ai. Do not submit candidates unless NSFW comfort has been explicitly confirmed on a call.

🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Team: Hands-on role reporting directly to Robin Razzi (QA Lead), who also brings solid hands-on security experience and can go deep with them on technical topics. Michael Monin (CTO & Co-founder) is also a stakeholder on this hire. That said, the candidate will be the sole dedicated security hire — they need to be comfortable owning the function independently day-to-day.
  • The Scope: Broad security generalist seat spanning Application Security, GRC, Security Awareness, IT/Access Management (MDM, provisioning), and emerging AI-specific threats — not a single-specialty role.
  • On-call Coverage: Owns monitoring of security channels/alerts, including occasional nights & weekend monitoring/on-call coverage (upon agreement with the team lead), so nothing goes unanswered.
  • NSFW: Candy.ai — an AI companionship platform which includes NSFW content, confirmed with every candidate before submission.

🎯 Calibration Anchors & Target Profiles

Ideal background:

  • Application/product security engineers from AI startups that scaled up (50+ employees, ideally 100–500 person scale-ups)
  • Comfortable being the sole or most senior security voice
  • 2–3+ years hands-on security experience — application security, GRC, or a mix; flexible on years given AI-startup intensity (e.g. ~1 year at Candy can weigh like ~3 years elsewhere)
  • Unconventional "hacker" mindset — someone who spends a significant amount of time independently experimenting, breaking things, researching vulnerabilities, and building original security projects outside of formal processes.
  • Hands-on use of AI tools (for code review, vulnerability research, or workflow automation) is a strong positive signal — useful evidence of the "hacker" mindset above, but not required.

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

  1. What were your main responsibilities in your previous security roles?
  2. Have you worked with external security auditors or penetration testing firms before?
  3. Walk me through a security risk or vulnerability you handled (explain it to me as if I'm not technical).
  4. What tools or platforms have you used for vulnerability management, code review, or GRC/risk tracking?
  5. If you joined a company with no risk register or documented risks, how would you start? (Note the answer for technical review, no need to judge quality yourself.)
  6. Are you comfortable with a role that includes occasional nights & weekend monitoring/on-call coverage?
  7. Have you seen Candy.ai? Our product operates in the AI companionship space and generates adult/NSFW content — are you fully comfortable working with this content on a daily basis?
