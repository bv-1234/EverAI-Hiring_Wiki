🎯 Calibration Anchors & Target Profile

🔗

See 🚩 Candidate Hard Gates & Red Flags for company-wide screening rules (NSFW, B2B/location defaults, compensation-vs-history, availability, ownership & evidence, communication, AI-usage). Below is what's specific to this role.

⛔️ Non-Negotiables

  1. Proven commercial CRM impact: Must demonstrate a major improvement they personally drove in CRM. Require a clear baseline, actions, ownership, timeframe, final result, and explanation of how they proved CRM caused the improvement.
    🚩 Generic claims such as "improved engagement" or "worked on retention" are insufficient.
  2. Relevant B2C experience at scale: Must have worked in a high-volume consumer business built around subscriptions, repeat deposits, tokens, credits, virtual currency, or another recurring-purchase model where CRM directly influenced revenue.
  3. Hands-on lifecycle ownership: Must have personally built segments, triggers, campaigns, experiments, and automated journeys covering both first-purchase conversion and repeat monetization.
    🚩 Reject strategy-only, newsletter-only, deliverability-only, or VIP-management-only profiles.
  4. Ownership and problem-solving: Must be comfortable building without a finished playbook, identifying the highest-value opportunities, working across teams, and creating momentum without waiting for perfect information.
  5. NSFW comfort: Must be fully comfortable working with uncensored models and adult-oriented content.
    🚩 Any hesitation is a rejection.

✨ Strong Additional Signals

  • Increased CRM from a minor channel into a meaningful revenue contributor
  • Built or significantly redesigned a CRM function
  • Experience in iGaming, free-to-play gaming, or another repeat-purchase model
  • Experience leading or coaching CRM specialists
  • Uses AI for analysis, segmentation, personalization, experimentation, or content development

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

❓ Interview Questions

  1. What is your current situation, and why are you considering a move?
  2. What attracted you to EverAI and this opportunity? Did you review Candy.ai and the Handbook — how deeply did you explore the product?
  3. Are you fully comfortable working with uncensored models and adult-oriented content?
  4. In your current or most recent role, what were your actual CRM goals and primary success metrics?
  5. What was the company's business model and user scale? Approximately how much revenue did CRM influence or generate?
  6. What is the most significant CRM achievement you personally delivered, ideally within the past 6–12 months?
  7. How did you measure that impact, and how did you establish that CRM — not another factor — caused it? Did you use holdouts, control groups, or another incrementality method?
  8. Which channels, CRM platforms, segmentation logic, and campaign workflows do you personally manage?
  9. What are your future career goals?
  10. Why did you leave each of your last two or three companies?
  11. Notice period, earliest possible start date, and any competing commitments?
  12. What questions do you have for me?
