🎯 Role Purpose

Own paid acquisition for CandyAI, from campaign execution through profitable growth.

These are hands-on roles. Performance Marketers personally operate campaigns, make budget decisions, analyze commercial performance and contribute to creative testing.

Managing teams, agencies, clients or partnerships does not replace direct campaign execution.

🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Working Environment

  • High-volume B2C subscription product.
  • More than $10M in monthly marketing spend across the business.
  • Small teams with significant individual ownership.
  • Fast testing and decision cycles.
  • International campaigns and audiences.
  • Fully remote.
  • NSFW product.
  • B2B contractor arrangement.

📊 What Successful Hires Had in Common

The analysis of our three successful hires identified four repeated characteristics.

  • Current execution: they personally operated acquisition campaigns. Their execution was recent and was not limited to strategic supervision.
  • Personal control: they could clearly identify the budgets, campaigns, platforms and decisions they personally controlled.
  • Commercial impact: they connected their decisions to profitable spend, subscriptions, revenue, CAC, LTV, ROAS or payback.
  • Creative ownership: they contributed directly to creative hypotheses, testing decisions, winner selection and iteration.

The required scale and depth differ by seniority, but these four characteristics apply to every Performance Marketer.

Agency profiles

Agency candidates can be strong when they personally operate client campaigns and can isolate their own decisions and results.

Do not progress candidates based only on:

  • Total agency portfolio.
  • Client-service experience.
  • Account management.
  • Strategic recommendations.
  • Work executed by another media-buying team.
