📊 What Successful Hires Had in Common

The analysis of our three successful hires identified four repeated characteristics.

  • Current execution: they personally operated acquisition campaigns. Their execution was recent and was not limited to strategic supervision.
  • Personal control: they could clearly identify the budgets, campaigns, platforms and decisions they personally controlled.
  • Commercial impact: they connected their decisions to profitable spend, subscriptions, revenue, CAC, LTV, ROAS or payback.
  • Creative ownership: they contributed directly to creative hypotheses, testing decisions, winner selection and iteration.

The required scale and depth differ by seniority, but these four characteristics apply to every Performance Marketer.

Shared requirements

Every candidate must have:

  1. B2C or DTC acquisition experience, ideally at a high-volume subscription business with $10M+ in monthly marketing spend.
  2. Recent hands-on campaign execution.
  3. Clear personal ownership — comfortable in small teams with significant individual ownership.
  4. Understanding of commercial performance metrics.
  5. Clear communication.
  6. Comfortable with fast testing/decision cycles, international campaigns and audiences, and fully remote work.
  7. Full comfort working for an NSFW product.
  8. Acceptance of the B2B contractor arrangement.
