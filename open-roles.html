Our Open Roles (+ Briefs & Pre-Screen Questions)

🚩

Before reviewing any role: read the shared Candidate Hard Gates & Red Flags guide. These rules apply across EverAI unless the role brief explicitly says otherwise.

ℹ️

This page lists all roles we are actively hiring for at EverAI — the company behind Candy.ai, one of the leading AI companion platforms in the adult space.

We work with a select group of agency partners to source talent across R&D, Growth, and Operations.

Before submitting candidates, make sure you have read this guide.

🎯 How to Approach Submissions

  • Quality > Volume: One perfectly matched candidate is worth more to us than ten "maybe" profiles.
  • Check the Off-Limits List: Ensure your candidates are not currently employed by our restricted partners, on this list here.
  • If unsure whether a candidate is a fit, ask your TA contact before submitting

✋ Submission Quality Bar

ℹ️

IMPORTANT
We strongly prefer fewer, high-quality candidates over volume.

Before clicking "Submit" in Ashby, run every candidate through this final checklist:

  • The "Doer" Test: Does this person actually build things, or do they just "manage" processes? We only hire hands-on executors.
  • Budget Alignment: Are they strictly within the ranges listed in the table below?
  • Location & Deel Logic: Does their location align with our B2B vs. EOR preference? (Check this page before submitting).
  • NSFW Comfort: Have you explicitly confirmed they are comfortable working for an adult / explicit-content product? Direct exposure frequency is role-specific — see the role brief and 🚩 Candidate Hard Gates & Red Flags.
❌

If the answer to any of the above is no → do not submit.
