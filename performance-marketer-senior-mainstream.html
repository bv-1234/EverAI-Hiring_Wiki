⛔ Requirements

  1. Hands-on paid acquisition: currently launches and optimizes campaigns personally. Strategy-only or agency-management-only profiles do not qualify.
  2. $300K+/month personally controlled spend: budget the candidate personally allocates and optimizes on mainstream platforms. Team, company, client-portfolio, or agency totals do not count.
  3. Commercial impact: a recent result tied to CAC, ROAS, LTV, or payback, with what they personally changed to get it.
  4. Creative-testing ownership: personally decides what to test, scale, and stop — not just receiving creatives from another team.
  5. Relevant B2C acquisition: proven consumer-acquisition experience where conversion and unit economics matter (subscription/DTC/app). General marketing, product marketing, partnerships, social, account management, affiliate management, or demand-gen roles don't count unless the candidate personally operated acquisition campaigns.

💬 Screening Questions

  1. What's your current situation, and why are you considering a move?
  2. Are you comfortable working for an adult-AI companionship company day-to-day? This seat doesn't run Adult RTB or explicit-creative execution directly.
    • Have you worked with NSFW/adult content professionally before?
  3. Which platforms do you personally manage today, and what monthly budget do you directly control?
    • How much of that is personally yours vs. team/portfolio/company spend?
  4. Walk me through your creative-testing process.
    • What decisions do you personally make? How do you decide what to scale or kill?
  5. Give me one recent example where you personally changed spend or campaign strategy based on CAC/CPA/ROAS/LTV/payback.
    • What did you change, and what happened?
  6. What questions do you have for me?

🔎 Sourcing

⚠️

Target companies are a sourcing aid, not proof of fit. Every candidate must still pass the Requirements above.

Strong signals: direct execution on Meta, TikTok, Google, Instagram, or Reddit; multi-market acquisition; high-volume creative testing; consumer-subscription economics; experience scaling web funnels; experience navigating ad-platform restrictions or account bans on a regulated/sensitive product; cross-channel budget allocation; tracking/attribution tooling (GA4, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Voluum).

✨ Preferred: $1M+/month personally controlled spend; multiple mainstream platforms; international/multi-market acquisition; high creative-testing velocity; subscription-funnel and cohort economics.
