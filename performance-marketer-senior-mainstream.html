⛔️ Top Requirements

  1. Hands-on paid acquisition execution.
  2. $300K+/month personally controlled spend.
  3. Recent measurable commercial impact.
  4. Personal ownership of creative testing/iteration.
  5. Relevant B2C / consumer acquisition background.

🎯 Role Mission

Own and scale mainstream paid acquisition across major consumer platforms while protecting CAC, LTV, payback and subscription profitability.

📋 Responsibilities

  • Own campaigns across mainstream acquisition platforms.
  • Control and reallocate substantial advertising budgets.
  • Scale campaigns, audiences, markets and creatives.
  • Improve CAC, ROAS, LTV, payback and subscription revenue.
  • Diagnose performance changes across the advertising and product funnel.
  • Build structured creative-testing programs.
  • Establish testing budgets, winner rules and stopping rules.
  • Work with creative teams to increase testing velocity.
  • Navigate platform restrictions associated with sensitive products.
  • Communicate commercial decisions and results clearly.

⛔️ Screening Criteria

  • Is personally running and optimizing campaigns now.
  • Personally controls meaningful spend — Senior Mainstream target is $300K+/month personally controlled.
  • Can show recent measurable commercial impact.
  • Personally participates in creative testing and iteration.

Team budget, client portfolio value and company revenue do not count as personally managed spend unless the candidate personally controlled allocation and optimization. Years in marketing ≠ years in paid acquisition — general marketing, product marketing, partnerships, social media, account management, affiliate management, or demand generation do not count toward this bar unless the candidate personally operated acquisition campaigns.

Also confirms current operating experience (still close to execution — strategy/advisory/account-management-only is not suitable), proven company experience at meaningful scale, personal campaign ownership, and product-category alignment (fully comfortable with uncensored products and explicit advertising creatives).

🚩 Red Flags

🚩
  • Team/company/client portfolio numbers presented as personally controlled spend.
  • Vague "performance improved" with no numbers.
  • No current hands-on execution — has moved completely away from platform execution.
  • Creative entirely owned by another team; cannot explain creative-testing logic.
  • Execution is delegated to agencies or junior team members.
  • Success is described only through platform metrics; seniority masks limited operating depth.

💬 Recruiter Screening Questions

  1. What is your current situation, and why are you considering a move right now?
  2. What specifically attracted you to this opportunity with EverAI?
  3. Did you review Candy.ai and the Handbook?
    • What did you notice, and how far did you test the product?
  4. Are you fully comfortable working with explicit content as part of your day-to-day role?
    • Have you worked with NSFW or adult content in a professional context before?
  5. In your last role at [company], what were your actual goals, and what was the primary metric used to determine whether you were performing well?
  6. What is your biggest growth / technical achievement from the past 6–12 months?
    • What measurable business impact did this achievement have?
    • What was the actual performance metric?
    • Was it a company goal?
  7. Which platforms do you personally manage today, and what monthly budget do you directly control?
    • If unclear: How much of that is personally yours vs. team / portfolio / company spend?
  8. Walk me through your creative-testing process.
    • What decisions do you personally make?
    • How do you decide what to scale or kill?
  9. Give me one recent example where you changed spend or campaign strategy based on CAC / CPA / ROAS / LTV / payback.
    • What did you change?
    • What happened?
  10. What are your future career goals?
  11. Why did you leave each of your last two or three roles?
  12. Location / compensation expectations / notice period / B2B?
  13. What questions do you have for me?

🎯 Track-Specific Guidance

Strong signals: direct execution on Meta, TikTok, Google, Instagram or Reddit; multi-market acquisition; high-volume creative testing; consumer-subscription economics; experience scaling web funnels; experience navigating restrictions for sensitive products; cross-channel budget allocation. Named experience with Meta, TikTok, Google Ads/UAC, Snapchat, or native advertising; tracking/attribution experience (GA4, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Voluum); experience handling advertising restrictions, compliance issues or account bans.

Example of ideal/good candidate for calibration: this LinkedIn profile.

✨ Preferred: $1M or more in personally controlled monthly spend; experience across multiple mainstream platforms; international or multi-market acquisition; high creative-testing velocity; experience with subscription funnels and cohort economics.
