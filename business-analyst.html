🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 Team & Environment Context

  • The Role: A hybrid analyst/operator seat — some weeks are spent modeling a decision, others are spent in the day-to-day of the business, unblocking processes and coordinating across teams. Works directly with the founders and exec team on strategy, operations, and growth — owns analyses end-to-end, builds financial models and KPI dashboards, and runs special projects. Uses Metabase and Excel daily (Metabase not required going in). Full task breakdown is in the candidate-facing JD.
  • The Bar: A high-visibility role for someone who wants the pace and breadth of consulting with the ownership of an operator, where they'll directly impact an AI product already used by 50M real users. Comfort with ambiguity and a bias for action: scopes their own work and drives it independently in a remote setting. At EverAI, that means working directly with founders and execs without the layers of a typical Big4 team.
  • Growth path: Direct exposure to the executive team and a real influence on company strategy, with increasing scope and seniority as the company scales.
  • Location: Worldwide. Asia or Europe preferred — a preference, not a hard requirement; a candidate based elsewhere is still eligible.

🎯 Calibration Anchors & Target Profiles

⛔️ Non-Negotiables

  1. NSFW: Genuinely comfortable working on the business side of an adult-content product
  2. Bachelor's degree in business, economics, engineering, finance, or another relevant quantitative field
  3. Consulting background: Big4 or middle-tier consulting — Strategy or Operations practice, 2–4 years, still Analyst/Associate/Consultant level, not yet Senior.
  4. Strong analytical toolkit: structured problem solving, financial modeling (P&Ls, unit economics, ROI/payback logic), subscription-business metrics literacy (MRR/ARR, CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio, retention/cohort analysis), and advanced Excel/Google Sheets (pivot tables, complex formulas, VLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH, sensitivity tables)
  5. Excellent communication + Top-level English: able to distill complex analyses into crisp narratives and slides for senior audiences — leads with the answer, then backs it up with evidence — and can independently build an executive-ready PowerPoint deck, not just fill in someone else's template, while managing a working relationship with a Manager or senior stakeholder and staying fully fluent, spoken and written.
💡

Tip for recruiters: case-style, structured thinking — the core consulting skill — is super important to assess in this profile. In practice: can they list out the possible reasons behind a problem, say what data would prove or rule out each one, and work through it out loud.

✨ Nice-to-have

  • At least one promotion within the Analyst/Associate/Consultant band during their tenure — a strong positive signal. Not required on its own; if absent, explore why during screening (e.g. recent hire, firm's promotion cycle timing).
  • AI fluency isn't required — genuine curiosity about AI tools is a strong positive signal
  • Exposure to tech, AI, or high-growth startup environments (companies/industries)

    • Analyst

    • Associate
    • Consultant
    Exact naming varies by firm (e.g. Deloitte: Analyst → Consultant; PwC/Kearney: Analyst → Associate → Senior Associate; EY/KPMG: Consultant → Senior Consultant) — screen for level, not the literal title string.
  • Job titles to avoid — signal someone already past the Analyst/Associate band, too senior (and too expensive) for this seat even if the firm and tenure otherwise fit:
    • Senior Consultant
    • Senior Associate
    • Engagement Manager
    • Manager
    • Principal, or anything above
🚫

Do not move forward Audit, Tax, M&A due diligence, or financial-advisory backgrounds — even at Big4, these are a different skillset entirely and don't transfer.

Do not source from McKinsey, BCG, or Bain (MBB), at any tenure — too expensive for this seat.

💬 Screening Questions

ℹ️

Use these questions during your screening call. Include a summary of the candidate's answers in your submission notes in Ashby.

  1. Our flagship product is an adult-content AI companionship platform. Are you fully comfortable working on the business side of that day-to-day?
  2. What's the achievement from your current role you're most proud of?
  3. Walk me through a specific business problem you solved recently — what was the actual ask, the process, and the outcome?
  4. Tell me about a time a client or stakeholder pushed back hard on your recommendation and you were sure you were right — what did you do?
  5. A client's revenue is declining 15% year-over-year. Walk me through how you'd approach figuring out why.
  6. Why are you looking to leave consulting now?
  7. What are the top 2–3 things you're looking for in your next role?
